# Hi, I'm Lilico.

**Aspiring SOC Analyst | Blue Team & Network Security**

I'm a self-taught cybersecurity learner currently focused on **Security Operations Center (SOC) Level 1** skills. I learn by investigating evidence, asking why something is suspicious, and documenting what the data actually supports.

My primary interests are **network traffic analysis, phishing investigation, incident triage, and defensive detection**. I am working through the TryHackMe SOC Level 1 path and building a portfolio of practical investigations.

## What I've practiced

- **Network traffic analysis:** Used Wireshark and NetworkMiner to inspect packet captures, follow conversations, apply display filters, and investigate DNS, HTTP, TCP, and other network activity.
- **Suspicious-traffic investigation:** Practiced analyzing scanning patterns, DNS TXT records, possible DNS or ICMP tunneling, cleartext credentials, and ARP anomalies in guided labs.
- **Phishing and email triage:** Examined sender metadata, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, links, redirects, and social-engineering indicators; documented uncertainty rather than assuming a phishing verdict.
- **Detection fundamentals:** Completed a Pyramid of Pain lab examining how hash, network, and behavioral detections differ in resilience.
- **Systems and foundations:** Practiced Linux command-line work, basic Windows/Linux concepts, and TCP/IP networking in hands-on environments.

These are **lab and independent-study experiences**, not claims of professional SOC employment.
